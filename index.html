SOCIAL NLP · GENDER & CULTUREFirst author · Under review at ARR

Gender Bias in LLM Social Judgment

Designed a gender-counterfactual evaluation across four LLMs and 19 cultural personas, separating leniency toward female protagonists from protectiveness toward female counterparts.

↳Favorable gender bias is driven more consistently by harsher judgments when the affected party is a woman.

Dolphins and whales

My first project out of high school explored sperm whale bioacoustics: classifying patterns of clicks called codas and distinguishing different whale dialects. A repeating 1 + 1 + 3 pattern caught my attention. Was it “hello” in whale? A fun speculation, not a translation.

Then I turned whale codas—and later dolphin whistles—into short musical compositions. They sound very silly. Not everything is art. But this is the project that got me interested in computer science and machine learning.
